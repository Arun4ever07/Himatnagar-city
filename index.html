CITY OVERVIEW

Situated on the picturesque banks of the Hathmati River, Himatnagar is a historically affluent city in northern Gujarat. Originally known as Ahmednagar, it was renamed in honor of Prince Himmat Singh of Idar. Today, it stands as a bustling gateway to North Gujarat, celebrated for its massive ceramic tile clusters, heritage stepwells, warm hospitality, and vibrant local trade.
